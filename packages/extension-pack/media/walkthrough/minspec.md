# MinSpec — Specification-Driven Development

Use **MinSpec** when you want to plan and track work: classify a task's tier (T1-T4),
write just enough spec for that tier, and follow its phases through to implementation.

MinSpec ships its own "Get Started with MinSpec" walkthrough with the full 5-step tour —
open it from here rather than duplicating it in this pack.

[Open MinSpec's Get Started walkthrough](command:workbench.action.openWalkthrough?%22aiclarity.minspec%23minspec.gettingStarted%22)

If the command above doesn't do anything, MinSpec may not be installed or active yet —
open the Extensions view, select **MinSpec**, and its walkthrough is on the extension's
own page.

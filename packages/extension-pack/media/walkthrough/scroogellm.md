# ScroogeLLM — cost measurement instruments

**Current status:** ScroogeLLM is shelved as a *product* (accepted decision, ScroogeLLM
DR-021) — there's no billing or go-to-market work happening on it. What stays live are
its measurement instruments: the tee-proxy, shadow classifier, safe-cut, and
scrooge-delegate. If you're looking for a cost-tracking dashboard or billing feature,
it isn't there yet, and this pack won't overstate it as one.

ScroogeLLM keeps its own local Decision Register and its own in-extension walkthrough,
if one is installed — open the Command Palette and search for "ScroogeLLM" to see what's
available in your installed version.

# DR-081: What ceremony a solo approval carries

## In plain terms - what this changes for you

Two things, both about removing ceremony that only ever made sense for a team, without
removing anything a machine can check.

1. The work that gets rid of the "Open PR" toast after `Alt+A` (SPEC-050) is **not** parked
   by the solo-mode cut. It was swept in by a filing error, not a decision.
2. An approval PR - the little `chore(approve): SPEC-NNN` one - stops being sent to the AI
   reviewer, because there is nothing in it to review. A cheap deterministic check takes its
   place, so the PR can go green **honestly** instead of needing `--admin` every time.

Net effect: fewer clicks per approval, and one fewer standing reason to bypass a gate.

## Context

**DR-075 / DR-076 (accepted 2026-08-01)** pivoted MinSpec to solo-first and cut or parked the
ceremony that exists to coordinate multiple humans. DR-076's park list includes:

> | Docs-lane / presence-gated ff / session mailbox (DR-051/DR-065) | **PARK behind team profile** - worktree-per-concurrent-agent discipline stays (that one defends against agents, not humans) |

(`DR-076.md:78`)

The reasoning is sound *for what DR-076 was looking at*: the docs-lane as **human-coordination**
machinery. But SPEC-050 uses the same lane for the opposite purpose - as the **solo** landing
route for an approval, so one person needs no git steps at all. DR-076 never considered that
use, and SPEC-050 never cited DR-076. The collision is an omission on both sides, not a
disagreement.

SPEC-050 being filed under `epic: EPIC-009` ("Team Readiness") is what made the collision
invisible. EPIC-009's own membership test is *"Team features only have meaning when >1 actor
shares the repo"* (`EPIC-009-team-readiness.md:26-27`), which SPEC-050 fails.

**Separately**, approval-record PRs cannot currently pass their own required checks.
`ai-review` is required and runs `on: pull_request` only, so it does execute - but the payload
is a generated sidecar plus a `status:` flip, with no prose and no logic to review. The
observed outcome is that these PRs merge on `--admin`: #1365 was created 02:47:41 and merged
02:47:55 with three required checks red and `ai-review` never reporting. Constitution
invariant 2 forbids exactly this shape - *"no required check hinges on a single producer that
one permission/config gap can disable."* Today the producer is the maintainer's admin
keystroke.

The precedent for the fix already exists. `ai-review` has been three-way since #480:

> machinery PRs (touches `.github/`/`scripts/`) → `neutral` (self-exempt, a gate can't certify
> itself), `ai-review:pass` → `success`, anything else → `failure`

(`ai-review.yml:139`)

`neutral` is an established way for a PR shape to satisfy a required check without a false
green.

## Decision

**1. SPEC-050 is carved out of DR-076's docs-lane park.**
The park stands for the docs-lane as human-coordination machinery. It does **not** extend to
SPEC-050's use of the lane as the solo approval-landing route. Whoever applies the solo-mode
cut (#1169) must leave that path live.

**2. SPEC-050 is re-homed from EPIC-009 to EPIC-006 (Trust, Consent & Supply Chain).**
Its contested surface is FR-8, standing push consent, and DR-078 - the decision that governs
it - is already EPIC-006. Materialized by #1377, which also flags that `epic:` is not stripped
by canonicalization, so the edit voids SPEC-050's existing approval and needs a fresh one.

**3. An approval-record PR self-exempts from `ai-review`, returning `neutral`.**
Same mechanism machinery PRs use. Detection is **structural, never title-based**: the changed
set is exactly one sidecar under `.minspec/approvals/**` plus the approvable it points at, and
nothing else. A title is forgeable; a path set is not.

**4. A new required check, `approval-integrity`, replaces `ai-review` for those PRs.**
Deterministic, offline, no LLM. It verifies the sidecar's `specHash` equals the canonical hash
of the approvable **at that commit**; that `approvedBy` is a permitted human identity,
re-checked server-side because a local `assertHumanApprover` proves nothing about a pushed
branch; and that the changed-file set is exactly the approval pair, so nothing rides along
inside an exempted PR. It fails closed and visibly. Materialized by #1376.

**5. The exemption covers the RECORD, never the APPROVABLE.**
This is the load-bearing boundary. The spec, DR or epic still gets a full `ai-review` verdict
on its own content PR, before it can be approved. DR-047 exists because approvals were being
rubber-stamped and a retroactive audit found 7 live defects that human review waved through;
nothing here touches that substance gate. An implementation that weakens it is wrong even if
it satisfies §3 and §4.

## Consequences

- One fewer human action per approval, and `--admin` stops being routine. That is the real
  invariant-2 win: the bypass becomes exceptional again, so its use carries signal.
- `ai-review`'s three-way conclusion now has two exemption reasons rather than one. Each needs
  its own stated justification, or `neutral` decays into "the check we skip". §3 and §4 are
  written as a pair for that reason - the exemption never stands alone.
- SPEC-050's re-home voids its approval and forces a re-read. That is a real cost, paid once,
  and it is the correct behaviour of a hash-locked approvable rather than a defect.
- The `approval-integrity` check is new required surface. It must be built before the
  exemption ships, or approval PRs go from "red and bypassed" to "green and unchecked", which
  is strictly worse.

## Alternatives considered

**Park SPEC-050 along with the rest of the docs-lane.** Internally consistent, and it is what
a literal reading of DR-076:78 implies. Rejected because it parks the single highest-frequency
friction a solo maintainer feels, on the strength of a filing error - EPIC-009 membership -
rather than any property of the work.

**Re-scope EPIC-009 instead, and leave SPEC-050 in it.** Rejected as insufficient: the epic
question is real but separate, and answering it does not by itself tell #1169 whether the lane
survives. §1 answers that directly.

**Drop `ai-review` from the required set for all PRs.** Rejected outright - it removes the
gate DR-047 was written to install, for the convenience of one PR shape.

**Keep merging approval PRs with `--admin`.** Rejected. It works, which is the problem: a
bypass used on every approval is indistinguishable from no gate, and invariant 2 names this
shape specifically.

**Amend invariant 2 to permit a routine bypass.** Rejected. The invariant is the product.

## Costly to Refactor

The expensive-to-undo moment is **after** `approval-integrity` becomes a required check in the
branch ruleset. At that point every approval PR in flight depends on it, and a defect in it
blocks approvals repo-wide rather than merely annoying one. Build it, run it advisory on real
approval PRs, and only then make it required - the same staging #480 used for `ai-review`
itself.

Cheap to undo: §1 and §2. A carve-out and an `epic:` field are one-line reversals if the solo
profile later decides differently.

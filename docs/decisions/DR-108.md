# Delegated approval under the bot's own name

> **On this record's id.** The id is in the frontmatter and the file name, and the
> generated index carries it. The prose does not spell it, or the id of any sibling record
> from queue item ctg, because the parent-register gate reads any id at or above one
> hundred as a parent-register reference (#2148). This is the same workaround #2412 used;
> #2148 lists what to undo when the gate is fixed. The four sibling records are: the
> core-tenets record, the direction-vs-engineering record, the delegated-approval record,
> and the review-panel record. All four took consecutive ids, the next free across `main`
> and every open pull request on 2026-10-17.

> **Born `proposed` per DR-029.** Acceptance is a separate human act
> (*MinSpec: Accept ADR*), and this record edits the stop list, so it is DR-086 §2.6
> (edits the autonomy rules) as well as §2.2. The delegation it creates
> (`.minspec/delegations.json`, DLG-001) covers **nothing** until this record's `status:`
> is `accepted`, and the code checks that (`scripts/lib/autonomy.ts`,
> `evaluateDelegation`). No approval, acceptance or sign-off is recorded in the
> founder's name by this record or the change that carries it.

## In plain terms - what this changes for you

You said "greenlight all future development" for MinSpec. Today the rules cannot honour
that, because they treat "the bot pretends to be you" and "the bot acts on what you said,
under its own name" as the same thing. This splits them. Pretending to be you stays
banned, and `--admin` stays yours. The bot may approve specs and engineering decisions
under its own name (`minspec-sdd[bot]`), citing your words, but only after a second AI
has reviewed the work. memory-fabric stays yours. If the bugfix share starts climbing,
the delegation switches itself off.

## Context

The approval-or-acceptance stop class (DR-086 §2.2) says, in code
(`scripts/lib/autonomy.ts`, `STOP_CLASSES`): "The agent cannot sign these without forging
the human." That is true only while the approval record has no field for *who acted, on
whose delegation*. The rules-consent review (rules-consent-review-1017) separated two acts:

- **Impersonation**: a record naming the founder as the actor on an act he did not take.
  It protects the honesty of a public history and his credential, whatever his trust in
  the agents.
- **Delegation**: the bot acts and records itself as the actor, citing words he gave.
  Nothing about this is dishonest. DR-086 Amendment A (the autonomy grant) is already one.

The evidence that his keystroke checks little:

- 62 of 82 machinery merges came within 30 seconds of the one before, with no recorded
  catch in 30 days (issue #2813, the rubber-stamp audit).
- He says he rubber-stamps MinSpec and voip specs.
- The repeat-fix share is 6.7% (11 of 165 fix commits in 60 days), far from 333Method's 3:1.

## Decision

### 1. Impersonation stays banned, unconditionally

Never record the founder as the actor on an act he did not take, whatever consent was
given in chat. `--admin` stays his: it uses his credential and bypasses every check, so it
is impersonation and an irreversible act at once. A delegation clears the
approval-or-acceptance class and **nothing else**. `stopClassesAfterDelegation` can
remove exactly that one class, so this holds by construction.

### 2. Delegation is allowed, under a committed, scoped, expiring record

`.minspec/delegations.json` holds each delegation: the founder's verbatim words, the
date, the source, one repo, the acts and tiers covered, a review-by date, and a ratio
tripwire. The first entry, DLG-001, quotes queue item in5 and covers
`AIClarityAU/minspec` for spec approval and engineering-DR acceptance (the direction-vs-engineering record), all tiers,
with review by 2027-01-17. A delegation covers an approval only when **all** of these hold
(`evaluateDelegation`):

1. The repo is not memory-fabric. This is hard-coded, so no record can widen it.
2. The actor is exactly `minspec-sdd[bot]`.
3. An independent verdict exists, and it is neither the author's nor the recorder's.
   The second AI gives the verdict; the author never does.
4. For a DR acceptance, the record is `kind: engineering` (the direction-vs-engineering record). Direction records
   stay the founder's.
5. The approval touches none of: the constitution, the delegation records, the autonomy
   setting, `scripts/lib/autonomy.*`, DR-086, or this record.
6. The repeat-fix share is known and at or below the tripwire (13%, the trigger the
   monthly practice review already uses). An unknown share is not permission.
7. The covering delegation's decision record is `accepted`, and today is on or before
   its review-by date.

Agents may tighten or suspend a delegation, but never widen one.
`.minspec/delegations.json` is a boundary path in `scripts/auto-merge-gate.ts`, so no
edit to it auto-merges. The founder's merge keystroke is the witness.

### 3. Spec approval by blast radius (change 3)

Spec approval follows the delegation's scope, not a blanket rule. memory-fabric specs need
the founder. MinSpec specs do not, once DLG-001 is live. voip specs do not either, once
voip carries its own record (#2856). Constitution invariant 3 means this repo's record
cannot cover another repo. Under the founder's amendment to change 6, specs keep the
full AI review panel (the review-panel record), because the panel's verdict is now what a spec approval
rests on.

### 4. What does not change

- The ban on any GitHub write falling back to his account (the token guard).
- The rule that a queue item cannot carry consent. Any session can write the queue, so a
  delegation lives only in a committed record.
- The machinery-merge keystroke. It is replaced only when SPEC-065's machinery witness
  ships, not by this record.

## Rejected alternatives (DR-086 §4)

- **Record approvals under the founder's identity, citing his chat consent.** Rejected:
  that is impersonation, and it stays banned whatever he says in chat. A record that
  names him as the actor on something he did not do is a false signpost.
- **Keep the stop class as is.** Rejected: it spends his attention on a keystroke he has
  said he does not read, against core tenet 1, and it records judgements that
  never happened.
- **Let the delegation live in the queue or in memory.** Rejected: any session can write
  those. Only a committed record, reviewed and merged by him, can carry it.
- **Activate the delegation on merge of the record alone.** Rejected: the acceptance of
  this record is his explicit act, and keying activation on it means an agent writing the
  file activates nothing.
- **One delegation covering every repo on the machine.** Rejected: constitution
  invariant 3.
- **No tripwire.** Rejected: his words carried a condition ("provided you keep that ratio
  in mind"). A delegation that ignores its own condition is a wider grant than he gave.

## Consequences

- Once DLG-001 is live and the wiring (#2854) has landed, MinSpec spec approvals and
  engineering-DR acceptances stop reaching him.
- **Accepted risk:** a wrong approval lands without him. Only the AI review panel, the
  ratio tripwire and the weekly digest (the direction-vs-engineering record) catch it.
- The code is written and tested but **not yet consumed**: no caller asks
  `evaluateDelegation` today, so the stop class still stops every time until #2854 lands.

# DR-075: MinSpec is a solo-first personal tool - open-sourced as a gift, not a funnel

> **Born `proposed` per DR-029** - acceptance is a separate human act
> (*MinSpec: Accept ADR*). This record's live status is its frontmatter `status:`
> field, not this note.

## In plain terms - what this changes for you

MinSpec stops pretending to be a business and becomes what it already is in
practice: the guardrail you use daily so AI-built code does not rot the way
333Method did. That is its whole job now. Everything sized for a market - publish
deadlines, funnel logic, team-grade governance - loses its justification.

## Context

The trigger was a competitor scare (OmniRoute, 36k stars, free, MIT) that on
audit turned out NOT to occupy ScroogeLLM's chosen moat - its engineering is
cache-aware but its headline savings math is cache-blind (verdict with file:line
citations on scroogellm#120). The scan that followed (scroogellm#121,
minspec#1168) established the real picture:

- MinSpec's lane was always free-crowded: GitHub spec-kit 124.8k stars,
  OpenSpec 63.3k, BMAD 51.3k, all free. MinSpec never had direct revenue in the
  plan; its monetization was adjacent (ScroogeLLM, Pro pack).
- The one unoccupied paid position (honest counterfactual cost metering +
  measured delegation) has a reachable self-serve buyer, but the realistic
  part-time ceiling is single-digit-thousands MRR - a bad trade against the
  founder's incoming day job, and exposed to two fast-follow clocks (Anthropic
  native /cost; ccusage).
- MinSpec's genuine differentiators - hash-locked approvals with staleness
  invalidation, a deterministic tier floor, a never-wrong signpost - have zero
  occupants, and two incumbents carry open tickets asking for them
  (kirodotdev/Kiro#9435, Fission-AI/OpenSpec#169).

So the product thesis survives contact with the market; the business thesis does
not survive contact with a day job.

## Decision

1. **MinSpec's customer is the founder.** It is a solo-first personal tool whose
   ROI is the quality of the founder's own rebuilds (ColdForge, LeadForge, the
   frozen-project harvest), not revenue. Feature scope narrows to the enforcement
   core plus daily solo use; no parity-chasing with spec-kit/Kiro on methodology
   content, webview polish, or team surfaces.
2. **Portfolio monetization is closed.** No paid tier, no freemium plan, no
   billing work anywhere in the portfolio. ScroogeLLM is shelved as a product by
   its own register's DR-021 (scroogellm#122); Sealbox stays shelved
   (sealbox#35).
3. **Open-source as a gift, not a funnel.** Repos go public when convenient -
   after the readiness sweep (#1171) - with no publish deadline and no
   share-of-voice urgency. Marketplace publishing remains a separate,
   founder-approved act.
4. **Team mode is parked, not deleted.** MinSpec as it stands would force a team
   to change its ways of working wholesale; no team is asking. Team-only
   machinery is retained behind a future profile split (see DR-076) rather than
   ripped out, so the option stays cheap.
5. **The give-back channel is upstreaming, not maintaining.** The proven
   canonical-hash + staleness mechanism gets offered to OpenSpec's open
   discussion #169 (#1170) under the founder's own identity. One mechanism, one
   invitation, no product commitment.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Adopt a competitor as the base and build only the uncovered delta.**
  Considered seriously (founder asked). Rejected because the delta IS the core:
  the uncovered parts are exactly the enforcement machinery MinSpec has already
  built and runs daily, while the covered parts (methodology prose, templates,
  phase commands) are commodity we already have. Migrating working guardrails
  onto OpenSpec's artifact model to then rebuild hash-locking around their CLI is
  switching cost with no gain - and spec-kit is philosophically opposed
  (model-trusted prose vs our enforce-don't-trust). Upstreaming one mechanism
  (#1170) captures the entire give-back value without the migration.
- **Keep the freemium ScroogeLLM pursuit.** The arithmetic: most-likely $0,
  plausible $2-6k/yr part-time, against day-job hours. Closed.
- **Publish immediately for share of voice.** Without a funnel there is no star
  race and no clock. Publish when it is good, or never.

## Consequences

- DR-027's privatize-the-moat rationale is void once nothing is monetized;
  open-sourcing scroogellm becomes an option (its own decision + sweep,
  scroogellm#122).
- Public repos dissolve the proprietary-code objection to PRC-jurisdiction free
  tiers for agent work; the identity-risk constraint (never route Claude OAuth
  through a third-party proxy) still stands (scroogellm#123).
- The ceremony this repo runs on must shrink to solo scale: DR-076.

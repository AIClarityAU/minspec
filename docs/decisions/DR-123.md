# An agent's recorded (rec) pick closes T3 Clarify; sign-off ratifies it

> **On ids in the prose.** Record ids at or above one hundred appear only in the
> frontmatter, because the parent-register gate reads them in prose as parent-register
> references (#2148), the same workaround the delegated-approval record uses.

## In plain terms

When a T3 spec's Clarify questions each carry a recommended option, an agent may record
that option as the answer. Once every question has one, Clarify is closed. Approving the
spec, by the founder or under a covered delegation, ratifies those answers. Nobody has to
read the questions separately before sign-off.

## Context

The founder answered this as runner queue item clarsel on 2026-11-17 (words in
`triggered_by`). The delegated-approval record says in its section 4 that a queue item
cannot carry consent, because any session can write the queue. So the answer is not
binding until it sits in a committed record the founder accepts. This is that record.
**It binds nothing until he accepts it.**

The gap it closes. The ai-review panel blocked #3800 (SPEC-311) and #3801 (SPEC-305)
unanimously. Its reason: on a T3 spec, picks an agent copied from the recommended options
do not make Clarify `done`. #3104 adds a gate that refuses delegated approval of a T3/T4
spec until its Clarify is closed. Taken together, no T3 spec with agent-recorded Clarify
could ever pass delegated sign-off. The spec pipeline was full, 40 of 40 slots.

The repo's own Clarify instruction (`packages/minspec/src/lib/slash-commands.ts:147`)
already treats a recorded selection as closing a question. No reviewer role file,
constitution invariant or accepted record says otherwise. The panel inferred the
human-only reading from CLAUDE.md ("Per SDD FR-2: Clarify phase required for T3-T4") and
from the template line "no human chose this item".

## Decision

1. **Who closes it.** On a T3 spec, an agent's recorded pick of the option the spec marks
   (rec) closes that question. It is written in the "answered, not ratified" form from
   #3399: a `Recorded selection:` line naming the (rec) option and saying an agent copied
   it and no human chose it.
2. **Ratification.** Approving the spec ratifies every such pick. The approver may change
   any of them before approving.
3. **What stays open.** These still keep Clarify open, and reviewers still block a
   `clarify: done` claim over them:
   - a question with no recorded selection
   - a question that names no recommended option
   - a question the spec's own text keeps open on purpose (SPEC-225 holds one for the
     founder, SPEC-255 holds two until a new record exists)
4. **Scope.** T3 only. T4 is unchanged. This record is the founder's own decision, not a
   delegation. #3411 stands: a delegation covers approval only, never a Clarify answer.
5. **Not decided here.** Which phase field records a closed Clarify, and next to which
   `specify` value, is SPEC-320's open question (#3414). This record settles only who
   closes a question.

## Cost and risk

- A (rec) option the founder would have changed is ratified unread when he approves
  without reading. Under a delegation, nobody reads it at all. That is the cost he
  accepted ("no reading for you").
- Mitigations: every pick is labelled as an agent's in the spec text, so a later reader
  can see which answers no human chose. Any question with no (rec) option, or one the spec
  reserves, stays open.

## Rollback

Set this record `superseded`, then revert the CLAUDE.md paragraph that cites it (#3802).
The specs keep their recorded picks, so their Clarify reads as open to the panel again.
That takes under a day.

## Rejected alternatives (DR-086 §4)

- **Keep the human-only reading.** Rejected by the founder in the queue answer. It holds
  every T3 spec with agent-recorded Clarify out of delegated sign-off, and the pipeline
  was already full.
- **Extend a delegation to cover Clarify answers.** Rejected: #3411 keeps delegations to
  approval only, and the founder made this call directly instead.
- **Treat the queue answer as binding without a record.** Rejected: the delegated-approval
  record's section 4 bars a queue item from carrying consent.

## Consequences

- #3802 states the rule in CLAUDE.md for the ai-review panel. It cites this record and
  merges after acceptance.
- #3800 and #3801 can be re-reviewed once #3802 is on main.
- #3104 keeps refusing a T3/T4 spec whose Clarify is not closed.

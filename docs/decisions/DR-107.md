## In plain terms - what this changes for you

You stop accepting decision records about *how* something is built. You keep accepting
the ones about *what* MinSpec does and *who* decides. The engineering ones are accepted
when the independent AI review passes, and once a week you get a short list of what was
accepted, so you can pull any of them back.

## Context

DR-086 §2.2 (the approval-or-acceptance stop class) makes every DR acceptance a founder
stop. The register held 105 records by 2026-10-10, about five a week (core-tenets-review-1017),
and many decide mechanism rather than direction: DR-095 (socket bind-mount), DR-094 (token
lifetime), DR-077 (hook parity test), DR-097 and its successor on whole-panel vote reuse (2026-10). Under the core-tenets record
core tenet 1 (make the founder's job smaller) a gate must name the error only it catches.
For an engineering record that error is already caught by the independent AI review
(DR-033/DR-047), which DR-076 made the merge gate for exactly this reason.

## Decision

### 1. Every DR carries `kind: direction | engineering`

- **direction**: changes what the product does or promises, who decides or consents, the
  constitution, the stop list, the autonomy setting, a delegation, publishing or
  visibility, spend policy, or the portfolio. Accepted by the founder, as today.
- **engineering**: chooses a mechanism inside a direction already set. Accepted on an AI
  review pass under a live delegation (the delegated-approval record), recorded under `minspec-sdd[bot]`, never
  under the founder's identity.

A missing or unrecognised `kind:` reads as **direction** (fail closed). Any doubt is direction.

### 2. The author never classifies alone

The `kind:` an author writes is checked by the AI review panel. A voter that reads a
record as direction blocks an engineering label. That is a finding like any other.

### 3. A weekly digest

Engineering records accepted in the past seven days are listed for the founder once a
week, delivered inside a session (a reminder with a `cmd`, or the queue), never by email.
Each entry gives the id, the title, a one-line decision and the review verdict. Reverting
one is a direction act: he says so, and the record goes back to `proposed`.

### 4. Until the wiring lands, nothing changes

Engineering acceptance runs through the delegated-approval record's delegation, which is inert until the delegated-approval record is
accepted and its approval-record wiring (#2854) ships. Until then every DR still stops for
the founder.

## Rejected alternatives (DR-086 §4)

- **Keep every DR a founder acceptance.** Rejected: about five a week, mostly mechanism,
  and the review already gates merge (DR-076). This is the rubber-stamp layer the core-tenets record
  says to retire.
- **Accept engineering records with no digest.** Rejected: the digest is the only point
  where the founder can see a wrong engineering call before it compounds.
- **Classify by tier (T3/T4 = direction).** Rejected: tier is mechanical blast radius,
  not who decides. A T4 refactor can be pure mechanism, and a T2 wording change to the
  stop list is direction.
- **Default a missing `kind:` to engineering.** Rejected: the absent-value-means-permission
  shape, which DR-086's resolver refuses everywhere else.

## Consequences

- Founder acceptances fall to the records that set direction.
- **Accepted risk:** a wrong engineering decision lands without him. The AI review panel
  and the weekly digest are the only checks.
- Existing records need a one-time `kind:` classification (#2855). Until then, all of
  them read as direction, which is today's behaviour.
